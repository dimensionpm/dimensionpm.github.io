About Dimension Property Management

Dimension Property Management is a trusted real estate management company headquartered in Renton, Washington, serving property owners and tenants throughout the Pacific Northwest.

With more than 20 years of experience, Dimension PM specializes in residential and commercial property management including single-family homes, apartment communities, office buildings, and retail spaces.

Our team is committed to protecting property value while delivering a seamless and stress-free experience for both owners and tenants.
