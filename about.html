About Dimension Property Management

Trusted property management solutions across Washington State and the Pacific Northwest for more than 20 years.

Who We Are

Dimension Property Management is a professional real estate management firm headquartered in Renton, Washington, proudly serving property owners and tenants throughout the Pacific Northwest for more than 20 years.

What began as a commitment to simplifying property ownership in the local Renton market has grown into a full-service operation trusted by residential and commercial clients across Washington State.

Dimension PM was built on one simple belief — property owners deserve a management partner who treats every asset with the same care and professionalism as the owner would.

Our Team & Expertise

Dimension PM Renton is a dedicated team of real estate professionals with deep roots in the Washington rental market. Our staff brings combined expertise in leasing, tenant relations, property maintenance, financial reporting, and Washington State housing law.

We manage a growing portfolio of single-family homes, apartment communities, retail spaces, and office buildings throughout the Pacific Northwest.

At Dimension Properties, we understand that every property and every owner has unique goals. Our team creates customized management solutions that deliver long-term success for both owners and tenants.

What We Do

Dimension Property Management provides complete leasing and property services covering every stage of the rental process.

Tenant Screening

Thorough applicant screening for qualified and reliable tenants.

Lease Management

Fully compliant lease agreements under Washington State laws.

Maintenance Services

Fast repairs and maintenance through trusted local contractors.

Why Property Owners Trust Dimension Properties

Over 20 years of consistent service in the Pacific Northwest has given Dimension Property Management a strong reputation built on reliability, transparency, and results.

Property owners trust Dimension PM because we protect investments, reduce vacancies, and professionally handle every aspect of rental property management.

Tenants trust Dimension PM Renton because we maintain quality properties and provide responsive support with professionalism and care.
